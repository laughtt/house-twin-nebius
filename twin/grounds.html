What the duck can walk

It lifts its feet 1–2 cm. Pollen trains steps up to 1.5 cm.

Bigger steps become walls: on the map, and out of training. The duck asks to be carried.

Door sills go into training at 1–2 cm (assumed; LiDAR can't measure them).

Heights are medians of 50 cm tiles from the iPhone scan; ARKit drift and LiDAR noise are a few cm.
